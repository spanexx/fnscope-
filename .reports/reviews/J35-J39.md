### Judgement on the Kotlin bridge (`kotlin/mod.rs:44-47`) — SOUND, not UB

- **repr**: 0.25.10 `#[repr(transparent)] pub struct Language(*const ffi::TSLanguage)`
  (`tree-sitter-0.25.10/binding_rust/lib.rs:60-63`). 0.22.6 (the version pinned by
  `tree-sitter-kotlin 0.3.8`, `Cargo.toml:35`) has the identical
  `#[repr(transparent)] pub struct Language(*const TSLanguage)` (GitHub v0.22.6 source).
  A transmute between two transparent wrappers over a thin data pointer is bit-preserving.
- **`LanguageFn` repr is NOT a trap here**: `tree-sitter-language-0.1.9/src/language.rs:4-7`
  defines `LanguageFn` as a transparent wrapper over a *function pointer*, and
  `Language::new` *calls* it (`binding_rust/lib.rs:418-422`). The adapter does **not**
  transmute a `LanguageFn`; it transmutes the *result* of that call (a data pointer).
  Re-labelling that pointer is equivalent to the documented
  `unsafe { Language::from_raw(ptr) }` (`binding_rust/ffi.rs:34-40`). Had the new
  `Language` actually wrapped a `LanguageFn`, the bridge would be catastrophic UB — it does not.
- **ABI**: Kotlin grammar `LANGUAGE_VERSION 14`
  (`tree-sitter-kotlin-0.3.8/src/parser.c:15`), workspace range 13..=15
  (`tree-sitter-0.25.10/binding_rust/bindings.rs:3-4`; `set_language` check at `lib.rs:664-676`).
- **ownership/dealloc**: the transmute consumes the old `Language` (no second drop);
  the new value drops once through `ts_language_delete`
  (`binding_rust/lib.rs:626-630`), which is a no-op for non-WASM/static grammars
  (`tree-sitter-0.25.10/src/language.c:13-17`). No double-free, no premature free.
- Residual (non-UB) risks are recorded in Registration notes, not treated as a finding.
